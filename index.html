Weihang Zeng — MSc Architectural Computation, the Bartlett, UCL

I build generative systems that bring environmental performance, machine learning and spatial reasoning into the earliest stage of architectural design — voxel massing evolved under multi-objective optimisation, neural networks used as floor-plan generators, agent simulations translated into buildable form, and street-level computer vision applied to urban safety. Trained as an architect, I write the tools as well as the designs they produce.
